# Easy Strength for Aesthetics (ESA)

**Params:** 
- 5 lifting days/week + daily Zone 2.
- Heavy: hard, 3-5 reps with 0-1 RIR
- Medium: 6-8 reps with 1-2 RIR
- Assistance: 8+ reps, volume & pump
- Zone2: indoor cycling, carries, walking
- Weights 15-25', Zone2 20'-45'

**Weighted Chin-ups**
- Rep-pause (1 rep, 1-2'' rest, repeat) - minimum 6 reps
- Build to 7–8 total, work towards 6 continuous

**Assistance finishers**
- Rotate each session between pushups, sommersault squats, bicep curls
- Push-ups (50 reps in sets of minimum 10)
- Bicep curls (weight/reps pyramid, 5-10-15-10-5)
- Sommersault squats 32kg (30-50 reps)

**Zone 2**
20-45 minutes daily
- Bike
- Carries
- Garage walking
